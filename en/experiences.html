Teaching

I taught in the Computer Science Department at IUT de Laval as a part-time lecturer from 2022 to 2025 (64 teaching hours), then as a temporary teaching and research associate (ATER) from September 2025 to August 2026 (175 teaching hours).

Across all three years of the BUT, a French university bachelor’s degree in technology, I delivered lectures and computer-based practical sessions in databases, interface programming and virtual environment development.

Courses taught

BUT Year 1

Introduction to databases and SQL

  • SQL queries, data definition and manipulation.
  • Practical work with MySQL and phpMyAdmin.

Application development with user interfaces

  • Graphical interfaces and event-driven programming with JavaFX.
  • Procedural and declarative approaches with FXML.
BUT Year 2

SQL in a programming language

  • Procedural extensions, cursors and triggers.
  • Introduction to NoSQL with MongoDB.
  • Practical sessions with SQL Server Management Studio and Studio 3T.
BUT Year 3

Advanced development · Unreal Engine

  • Fundamentals of geometry, animation and rendering.
  • Project organisation (assets, levels, classes, etc.).
  • Working with actors (static meshes, geometry brushes, lighting, etc.).
  • The course focuses on Blueprints: programming fundamentals, followed by interactions (actors and characters), animation (actors only) and physics.

Teaching responsibilities

For some courses, I served as lead instructor, with the following responsibilities:

  • Updating lecture materials and practical exercises.
  • Preparing and delivering sessions.
  • Designing and marking examinations.

Student support

Internship supervision

As an academic supervisor, I supported students’ integration into companies and helped them address difficulties. I co-supervised reports with workplace supervisors and assessed reports, including those of other interns. I also served on third-year BUT oral defence panels and chaired some of them.

First-year tutoring

I provided individual support from the first month of the programme, reviewing students’ transition, discussing their experiences and identifying difficulties. Meetings at key points throughout the year maintained this support.

Outreach and guidance

Open days

I discussed the six-semester BUT programme, skills and career prospects with prospective students and their families. Student projects illustrated the programme’s project-based learning and assessment activities (SAÉ).

Science Festival

In 2024, I ran the Computer Science Department’s stand in Laval to introduce computing and its applications to the public.
