Context

My research focuses on designing and implementing models and tools for the automatic analysis and evaluation of technical gestures, addressing experts’ observation needs in the context of preclinical dental surgery training.

My research questions concern the adaptability of the automatic evaluation engine to experts’ evolving observation needs, as well as the open integration of multiple forms of motor expertise, with each teacher consulted individually and free to propose their own observation needs.

Contribution

Among my contributions, I developed VEEGO (Virtual Environment for the Evaluation of Gestures in Odontology), a virtual environment dedicated to the automatic evaluation of dental gestures. VEEGO captures motion, replays it through 3D avatars, and automatically identifies gesture and posture errors using machine learning models trained through its interface on expert demonstrations. It then provides learners with real-time visual and textual feedback on what to correct.

  1. Capture and replay motion

    • Streaming motion data.
    • Recording and replaying motion through 3D avatars.
    • Multiple capture systems: Qualisys, Perception Neuron and Azure Kinect.

  2. Train models through VEEGO

    • Segmenting and labelling expert demonstrations.
    • Computing motion descriptors.
    • Training a machine learning model for each observation need.

  3. Identify gesture and posture errors

    • Automatic real-time error detection.
    • Visual feedback through colour coding on a 3D avatar, alongside textual guidance.
    • A final summary of error occurrence frequencies over the recording.
    • Comparison of the learner’s gestures with a teacher’s demonstration.
Evaluated with more than 60 students

VEEGO was validated with experts and evaluated with more than 60 students at the Faculty of Dentistry in Nantes, France.

Students found VEEGO’s feedback useful for learning gestures and postures:

“It should be available to all students: very educational and instructive.”

EVAGO Project

This work is part of the EVAGO project ↗, in which I worked with dental experts from RMeS, education researchers from CREN and industrial partner HRV. The project is funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR) under its PRCE programme (ANR-21-CE38-0010 ↗).

I mainly contributed to WP3, led by LIUM, which aimed to propose models and prototypes to represent and evaluate activity according to experts’ needs. I also participated in other work packages.
